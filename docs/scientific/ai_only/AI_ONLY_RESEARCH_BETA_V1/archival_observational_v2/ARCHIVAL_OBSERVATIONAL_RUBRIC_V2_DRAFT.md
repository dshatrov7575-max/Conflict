# AI_ARCHIVAL_OBSERVATIONAL_V2 — DRAFT RUBRIC

Status: DRAFT_BEFORE_PRIMARY_CODING / SEPARATE FROM STRICT V1

This route exists because strict-v1 point anchors are speech-form anchors that the historical corpus usually cannot satisfy. Strict-v1 UNKNOWN records remain immutable. V2 does not pretend that demands, strikes or official actions are verbatim survey answers; it defines a separate archival content-analysis measurement.

## POS-AO2 [-10,-5,0,+5,+10]
Unit: narrow actor × projected PTN identity × exact reference statement × historical episode.

- -10: admitted actor evidence rejects the reference as a whole or rejects every essential component with an explicit substantial-change position.
- -5: admitted actor evidence is logically incompatible with at least one essential component of the reference and explicitly demands/requires substantial change, while whole-reference comprehensive rejection is not established.
- 0: actor explicitly states no directional preference toward the reference in the episode.
- +5: actor explicitly defends or preserves at least one essential status-quo component and rejects the corresponding change, while whole-reference comprehensive acceptance is not established.
- +10: actor explicitly defends the reference as a whole and rejects substantial change across its essential components.

No point is assigned from silence, headcount, source frequency, violence, outcome, emotional tone, analyst sympathy, or another actor's statement. Where incompatible admitted evidence for the same actor/time cannot be reconciled, use DISPUTED/null. Missing scope/time/attribution remains UNKNOWN/null.

## KVS-AO2 [0,2,5,8,10]
This is archival decision salience, not a psychological absolute.

- 0: actor explicitly states the issue is irrelevant/not a decision criterion in the documented context.
- 2: actor explicitly treats the issue as secondary to a named higher-priority issue and accepts concession on it.
- 5: the issue is a documented recurring/core demand or decision criterion across the actor's admitted episode evidence, but leading rank or settlement conditionality is not established.
- 8: the actor explicitly conditions continuation, refusal, settlement, participation or another consequential decision on resolution of this issue, without proving absolute non-negotiability.
- 10: actor explicitly makes satisfaction of the issue a non-negotiable condition and refuses the relevant settlement/option absent satisfaction.

Persistence alone, strike duration, number of participants, production loss, punishment severity, official duty, or repeated media coverage cannot create KVS. They may corroborate context only.
